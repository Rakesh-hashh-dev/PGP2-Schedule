Bridging the Campus Coordination Deficit: Transforming MBA Schedules into an Offline-First OS

Macro Context: Elite 2-year MBA cohort with 30+ electives, dynamic rescheduling, and strict 75-80% attendance compliance.

THE "AS-IS" CAMPUS TRILEMMA: HIGH FRICTION & RISK
Fragmented channels create cognitive overload and irreversible academic compliance risks.
1. Information Fragmentation
Raw Google Sheets & Cluttered WhatsApp Blasts
Discovery Latency: Schedules live in multi-tab spreadsheets. Sudden room or slot updates announced across 10+ WhatsApp groups cause students to spend 30–90s per lookup, frequently missing urgent changes.
2. Academic Penalty Risk
Strict 75–80% Mandatory Attendance Thresholds
De-Registration Threat: Missing just 2–3 classes risks course de-registration or grade drops. Students rely on error-prone mental math, leading to miscalculated absence buffers and severe academic penalties.
3. Coordination Paralysis
Cross-Elective Peer Scheduling Deadlocks
Collaboration Drag: Dispersed elective enrollment makes finding shared free windows for syndicate projects, case competitions, and committee work an iterative 15+ message WhatsApp ordeal.
THE "TO-BE" TRANSFORMATION: TERMFLOW ACADEMIC OS
A purpose-built Progressive Web App unifying scheduling, attendance, and peer networks.
CORE PRODUCT CONCEPT: Turnkey Academic Operating System
Integrates automated Google Sheets timetable diffing, predictive attendance margin calculation, and privacy-preserving peer schedule overlays into an installable mobile PWA.
Moat 1: Zero Admin Disruption
Transparent Google Sheets Synchronization
Zero Change Resistance: Academic offices continue publishing via existing Google Sheets. Apps Script & Supabase Edge functions poll, compute SHA-256 diffs, and push updates without requiring administrative re-training.
Moat 2: Offline-First Resilience
Sub-500ms Instant Boot Snapshot Engine
Basement-Ready Access: Pre-bundled build snapshots and Workbox service workers ensure 100% schedule availability in low-connectivity lecture hall basements. Live sync updates silently when reconnected.
Moat 3: Enterprise Trust & Zero Friction
Frictionless PWA + Domain-Gated Google Auth
Closed Campus Security: Authentication strictly gated to @iimsambalpur.ac.in via Clerk SSO. One-tap home screen install avoids app store friction; Row-Level Security (RLS) guarantees complete peer data privacy.
