What's new in TermFlow

Features, improvements and fixes — newest first.

v5.12
September 2026
Feature

SEO, AI discoverability & legal pages

  • ✦Privacy Policy & Terms of Service — public legal pages covering data collection, eligibility, and acceptable use.
  • ✦AI discoverability (GEO/LLMO) — enriched llms.txt, new llms-full.txt, expanded structured data with Organization, HowTo, BreadcrumbList, and speakable schema.
  • ✦About page expanded — 5 new FAQ entries (attendance tracking, calendar export, reminders, offline mode, eligibility), Quick Start section, and breadcrumb navigation.
  • ✦Offline fallback — branded offline page with auto-reload on connection restored.
  • ✦Vercel Analytics activated — privacy-first, cookie-free page analytics and Web Vitals monitoring.
  • ✦Security headers — Content-Security-Policy and Permissions-Policy added.
  • ✦Changelog — this page. Public release notes for all TermFlow versions.
v5.11
July 2026
Feature

Courses, Insights & Mess ratings

  • ✦Courses view — per-course cards with faculty name, room, credit hours, progress, and attendance status.
  • ✦Insights view — weekly recap with contact hours by day, subject-area split, attendance percentage, and busiest-day highlight.
  • ✦Deadline strip — "Next 7 days" exam and assignment rail on the Today view.
  • ✦Mess favourites — star dishes in the full menu modal; highlights carry across the app and a "favs today" pill appears in the mess card.
  • ✦Cancellation banner — in-app alert on Today/Tomorrow for your cancelled classes.
  • ✦Cancellation push notifications — opt-in alerts when one of your classes is cancelled (requires backend migration).
  • ✦Cohort mess ratings — thumbs up/down per meal with live aggregate (% liked · N ratings).
  • ✦PWA deep links — ?view=… query param switches view on load; home-screen shortcuts expanded.
