Product overview

The moving parts of your term, together.

TermFlow gives eligible PGP2 students a personal view of classes, attendance, deadlines and academic planning without making student information public.

Page updated 30 September 2026

Personal timetable

See daily and weekly classes shaped by enrolled courses, electives and sections, with schedule changes kept close to the affected session.

Attendance and leave planning

Record personal attendance, understand course-level risk and estimate how planned absences may affect your position. These calculations support planning and are not official records.

Exams, assignments and updates

Bring exam dates, assignment deadlines, announcements and recent schedule changes into the same student workspace.

Course learning

Keep course essentials, topics, after-class notes, revision items and self-checks organized around the course they belong to.

Planning tools

Use a private opportunity tracker, daily priorities and study-session planner. These planning records are stored locally in the browser unless the feature explicitly says otherwise.

Calendar and offline support

Export schedule events, configure reminders, install the progressive web app and inspect locally available data when the connection is unreliable.

What TermFlow does not replace

TermFlow is a planning aid. Institute announcements, official academic records and authorized administrative communication remain the source to follow whenever information differs.
