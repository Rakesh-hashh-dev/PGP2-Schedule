Privacy and access

Public product facts. Private student context.

TermFlow's public pages explain the product. Personal schedules, attendance, course selections and student workspaces are intended for eligible signed-in users.

Page updated 30 September 2026

What search engines can see

The student portal landing page, product overview, feature summary and this privacy explanation are public. They contain product information rather than student records.

What requires sign-in

Personalized schedules, attendance records, course selections, announcements, social features and administrative tools require eligible institute access.

Browser-local planning data

The opportunity tracker, daily goals, study-session plans and related preferences are stored in the browser on the device where they are entered. Clearing site data can remove them, so use the available backup export when needed.

Calendar subscription links

A calendar link contains the course and section filters selected for that feed. Treat the link as shareable schedule information: do not post it publicly, and remove it from your calendar application when it is no longer needed.

Search and AI crawlers

TermFlow permits search-discovery crawlers on public information pages while requesting that APIs, authentication callbacks and private app content are not indexed. Training-oriented crawlers may be restricted separately.

Corrections

Signed-in users can use the in-app feedback option to report incorrect product or schedule information. Do not include passwords, authentication codes or unnecessary sensitive information in feedback.

Planning information is not an official record.

When TermFlow differs from an institute notice, authorized communication or official academic record, follow the official source.
