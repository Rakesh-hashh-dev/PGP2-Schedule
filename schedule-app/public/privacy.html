Privacy Policy

How TermFlow collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Short version: TermFlow collects your name, email and schedule-related preferences only to personalise your academic dashboard. Your data is never sold or shared with third parties beyond the infrastructure providers listed below. You can request deletion at any time.

1. Who we are

TermFlow (pgp2.termflow.tech) is a student-built, privately operated web application for IIM Sambalpur PGP2 students (batch 2025–27). It is not affiliated with or operated by IIM Sambalpur administration.

2. What data we collect

  • Account information: Your name and email address, obtained through Google OAuth via Clerk when you sign in with your @iimsambalpur.ac.in account.
  • Preferences and selections: Your chosen electives, section, dashboard layout, theme preference, and sidebar pin configuration — stored so your personalised timetable is consistent across devices.
  • Attendance data: Class attendance records you confirm or update within the app, stored in Supabase.
  • Push notification subscription: If you opt in, your browser's push subscription endpoint — used only to deliver class reminders.
  • Usage telemetry: Anonymous, cookie-free page-view counts and performance metrics via Vercel Analytics. No personal identifiers are attached.
  • Error reports: Anonymised crash and error reports via Sentry, including browser version, device type, and stack trace. No personally identifiable information is intentionally included.
  • Local storage: Theme preference, startup name cache, and last-seen schedule data are stored locally in your browser. This data never leaves your device.

3. How we use your data

  • To display your personalised timetable, attendance, and deadlines
  • To authenticate you via your institute Google account
  • To send class reminders (only if you have opted in to push notifications)
  • To diagnose and fix crashes and errors
  • To understand aggregate usage patterns and improve the app

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to providing the TermFlow service.

4. Infrastructure providers

Your data is processed by the following third-party services:

  • Clerk (clerk.com/privacy) — authentication and session management
  • Supabase (supabase.com/privacy) — database (PostgreSQL hosted on AWS ap-south-1, Mumbai)
  • Vercel (vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy) — hosting and edge network
  • Sentry (sentry.io/privacy) — error monitoring
  • Google Apps Script — schedule data source (no personal data is sent; only a timestamp is included in schedule fetch requests)

5. Data retention

Account data and preferences are retained for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, your Supabase records (attendance, preferences, push subscription) are permanently deleted within 30 days. Sentry error reports are retained for 90 days per Sentry's default policy.

6. Your rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your stored data
  • Deletion: Request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Opt-out: Disable push notifications at any time from the app settings

To exercise any of these rights, email termflow.pgp2@gmail.com with "Privacy Request" in the subject line.

7. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Supabase row-level security (RLS) policies ensure that each student can only access their own records. Authentication is delegated entirely to Clerk's hardened OAuth infrastructure. We do not store passwords.

8. Cookies

TermFlow does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Clerk sets a session cookie required for authentication. Vercel Analytics is cookie-free. Local storage is used for performance caching only.

9. Changes to this policy

Material changes to this policy will be announced in the in-app announcements feed before taking effect. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: termflow.pgp2@gmail.com
