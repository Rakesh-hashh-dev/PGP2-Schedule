Quick answers

Frequently asked questions.

Answers to the essentials before you sign in.

What is TermFlow?

TermFlow is a private educational web app that brings an IIM Sambalpur PGP2 student's timetable, attendance, electives, exams, assignments and schedule updates into one place.

Who can use TermFlow?

Personal TermFlow features are available to eligible IIM Sambalpur PGP2 students who sign in with an @iimsambalpur.ac.in Google account.

What can students do in TermFlow?

Students can view a personalized daily timetable, track attendance, plan leave, follow exam and assignment deadlines, see announcements and schedule changes, and export events to a calendar.

Is TermFlow free?

Yes. TermFlow is free for eligible users.

Is student information public?

No. These public pages describe the product only. Personal schedules, attendance and course selections require authenticated institute access.

How does TermFlow track attendance?

TermFlow records the number of classes attended and calculates the attendance percentage for each course. You can see your current standing relative to the 75% minimum requirement. The Leave Planner lets you simulate how many future classes you can safely miss before falling below the threshold.

Can I export my class schedule to Google Calendar?

Yes. TermFlow supports calendar export. You can export your timetable as iCal events, compatible with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and other iCal-compatible apps.

Does TermFlow send class reminders?

Yes. TermFlow can send push notifications as class reminders. You can opt in and choose how many minutes before each class you want to be notified.

Does TermFlow work offline?

Yes. TermFlow is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Install it on your phone or desktop home screen and your most recently loaded schedule is cached — accessible even without an internet connection.

Is TermFlow available for PGP1 students or other programmes?

No. TermFlow is built exclusively for IIM Sambalpur PGP2 students (batch 2025–27). It is not available to PGP1 students, alumni, faculty, staff or external users.
