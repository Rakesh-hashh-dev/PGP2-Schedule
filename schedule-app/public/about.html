Frequently asked questions

What is TermFlow?

TermFlow is a private educational web app that brings an IIM Sambalpur PGP2 student's timetable, attendance, electives, exams, assignments and schedule updates into one place.

Who can use TermFlow?

Personal TermFlow features are available to eligible IIM Sambalpur PGP2 students who sign in with an @iimsambalpur.ac.in Google account.

What can students do in TermFlow?

Students can view a personalized daily timetable, track attendance, plan leave, follow exam and assignment deadlines, see announcements and schedule changes, and export events to a calendar.

Is TermFlow free?

Yes. TermFlow is free for eligible users.

Is student information public?

No. These public pages describe the product only. Personal schedules, attendance and course selections require authenticated institute access.
